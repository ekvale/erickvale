Math Bastion: The History of Ideas

A tower defense game where every tower is a mathematical idea, discovered — like the originals — because somebody needed it. Defend civilization from prehistory to the scientific revolution.

{% include "math_bastion/_game.html" %}

How to play

  • Pick a tower from the shop and click a free tile to build it (hold Shift to place several).
  • Click Send Wave when you're ready. Enemies that reach the far edge cost lives.
  • Click a placed tower to upgrade or sell it. Towers are fully refunded between eras — history moves the map.
  • Read the enemies: prime goblins shrug off ordinary damage but crumble to the Prime Watchtower; fraction slimes split when killed; composite golems decompose into their prime factors; negative spirits steal gold if they leak through.
  • Open the 📖 Discovery Codex anytime for the real history behind each idea.
