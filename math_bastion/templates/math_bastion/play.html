Math Bastion

An idle tower defense through the history of mathematics. One bastion, endless waves from every direction — and the only way into the next age of history is to pass its Math Trial, set by the people who actually discovered the ideas.

How to play

  • Your bastion fires automatically. Enemies attack from all directions; if they reach the walls, you lose health.
  • Spend coins in the ⚔ Attack, 🛡 Defense and 📖 Scholar tabs while the fight runs — small, constant upgrades win runs.
  • Every 10th wave brings a boss. Beat it, then pass the era's Math Trial (3 quick questions, no timer, retries allowed) to enter the next age and unlock new discoveries.
  • Read the enemies: prime shells shrug off damage until you research Prime Resonance; fraction slimes split when killed; composite golems break into their prime factors; negative spirits steal coins.
  • Falling ends the run, not the progress: you keep Wisdom for permanent Workshop upgrades, and 💎 gems can buy a Second Wind.
