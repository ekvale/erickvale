About the Collection

The MDH Publications Library is the central repository for reports, fact sheets, guidelines, research summaries, and other documents produced by the Minnesota Department of Health.

The library is organized around health topic areas and document formats, making it easy to find the information you need — whether you're a clinician, researcher, policymaker, or member of the public.

Content is continuously updated as new publications are released. Use the search and browse tools on the library home page to explore the full collection.

Access & Contributions

MDH staff can request contributor access to add or manage publications. If you've noticed an error or have a suggestion for improving the library, use the form on this page to let us know.
